# v0.2 supported domain (draft for the owner's decision)

**Status: DRAFT. Not approved.** Written 2026-11-22 against main at #322
(898ed69) by the v0.2 coordination agent, as the first open item of the
ledger's *Release qualification* row. That item stays open until the owner
approves or amends this page. Nothing here is a qualification claim: it
proposes which inputs v0.2 advertises, and it collects the evidence that
exists for each family at 898ed69.

The [acceptance ledger](v0.2-acceptance-ledger.md) remains the release
authority. Once approved, the approved domain should also be stated in the
manual ([`docs/intersection.rst`](../intersection.rst), *Outcomes*), which
describes what users can rely on. It is kept here until then, beside the
ledger, so that the manual does not advertise a domain the owner has not
decided.

## How to read this page

Each intersection family has three lists.

- **Supported**: a built-in handler answers with a complete result over
  the requested domains (ADR-0029, ADR-0010). A result with no component
  is a proved separation. The evidence is named; "local" marks evidence
  from a local run that CI has not repeated.
- **Fails closed by design**: the call returns a `KernelError`, never a
  partial or unproved result. The codes are `IntersectionFailed` (the
  search could not settle it), `CertificationFailed` (a branch could not be
  certified), `NotSupported` (input outside the handler's domain),
  `NotImplemented` (a case the handler names but does not yet answer) and
  `InvalidInput`. In v0.2 a refusal on this list is an accepted limitation.
  A wrong answer anywhere is a defect, never a limitation.
- **Out of scope for v0.2**: work deferred by the owner's decisions, which
  v0.2 neither answers nor gates on.

Questions for the owner are collected at the end.

## Common to every family

- **Built-in types.** Space curves: `Line`, `Circle`, `Ellipse`,
  `Hyperbola`, `Parabola`, `BSplineCurve` (`src/curve/Visit.cppm`). Plane
  curves: `Line2d`, `Circle2d`, `Ellipse2d`, `Hyperbola2d`, `Parabola2d`,
  `BSplineCurve2d`. Surfaces: `Plane`, `Cylinder`, `Cone`, `Sphere`,
  `Torus`, `Ellipsoid`, `BSplineSurface` (`src/surface/Visit.cppm`).
  B-splines may be polynomial or rational with positive weights.
- **Every ordered pair of built-in types has a handler**
  (`src/intersect/Dispatch.cppm`; `tests/intersect/DispatchTests.cpp`,
  "every built-in pair has a handler that names both types" and "every
  curve-surface pair has a handler"). Types that are not built in are
  `NotSupported`, and empty inputs `InvalidInput` (same file).
- **Outcome contract** (ADR-0029; `docs/intersection.rst`, *Outcomes*):
  success means complete, never "no seeds found". Branches over bounded
  ranges carry a certified deviation bound and its cause (ADR-0050 step
  0.2-T2). Overlaps and tolerant coincidences are reported as such.
- **Metamorphic relations** MR-01 to MR-04 (exchange, reversal,
  translation, scaling by powers of two) are exercised per family as
  listed below. The ADR-0053 release check still fails at #322 (ledger,
  *Mandatory metamorphic release evidence*).

## 1. Plane curves (2D curve–curve)

**Supported.** Every ordered pair of the six plane curve types.

- Lines against lines and conics, and two circles: every decision exact.
- Two conics: the exact chart quartic, crossings proved, tangencies of
  order two and four decided, coincident conics overlap.
- A B-spline against a line or a conic, by Bernstein subdivision; touches
  reported bracketed.
- Two B-splines: crossings proved by Krawczyk's test, including at or near
  interior knots, and placed on both curves to rounding (#316). Pairs far
  from the origin are searched moved to it. Tangencies of order two to
  four are reported bracketed, `Tangential` and undecided. Identical
  splines, and exact knot refinements of one another, overlap.

Evidence: `tests/intersect/PlanarTests.cpp`, `PlanarPairTests.cpp`,
`PlanarSplineKnotTests.cpp`, `PlanarSplineTangencyTests.cpp`,
`PlanarLinearizationTests.cpp`; `corpus/planar-curves/` (804 closed-form
cases in 28 families, `PlanarCorpusTests.cpp`); `docs/intersection.rst`,
*Plane curves*. 144 planar relation cells are `MR implemented` and await
review.

**Fails closed by design.**

- `IntersectionFailed`:
  - a B-spline along a conic;
  - a B-spline along another B-spline other than an identical or exactly
    refined copy;
  - a touch of two B-splines with no derivative of order up to four of one
    sign.
- `NotImplemented`: a B-spline along a line that is not monotone along it,
  or that the requested domains cut (`PlanarSpline.cpp`).

Reported but not decided, never dropped (`Decided` false): a touch of two
B-splines, a crossing at a spline's own domain end, and one at a knot that
no box proves single.

**Not yet evidenced:** OCCT differential tests (`Geom2dAPI_InterCurveCurve`)
and B2.1 timing.

## 2. Space curves (3D curve–curve)

**Supported.**

- Two lines meet within the Context's resolution, decided exactly on the
  input doubles (owner's decision, 2026-11-08). Exactly parallel supports
  within the resolution overlap. Rays and whole lines are supported.
- Two B-splines, polynomial or rational: meetings as critical points of
  the distance within the resolution, by subdivision with Krawczyk's
  test, including the domains' boundaries.
- Lines and conics against B-splines and each other, as enclosed Bézier
  pieces.

Evidence: `tests/intersect/LineLineTests.cpp`, `SplineCurvePairTests.cpp`,
`CurvePairTests.cpp`; `corpus/curve-curve/` (168 cases, both orders,
`CurveCorpusTests.cpp`) compared with OCCT's `Extrema_LocateExtCC` in
`tests/occt/IntersectionDifferentialTests.cpp`; `docs/intersection.rst`,
*Space curves*.

**Fails closed by design.**

- `IntersectionFailed`: tangent curves, and anything the search cannot
  settle (a line tangent to a circle, two circles equidistant all round,
  two descriptions of one curve).
- `NotSupported`: two unbounded domains (such as a whole line against a
  whole parabola or hyperbola); a circle or ellipse domain longer than one
  turn; line inputs outside the checked range of the exact degree-six
  predicates, since no rescaling is done.
- `CertificationFailed`: a line–line placement that cannot be bounded
  within the resolution.

## 3. Curves against elementary surfaces and the ellipsoid

**Supported.** Every built-in curve against plane, cylinder, cone, sphere,
torus and ellipsoid.

- A line against a plane: exact. Against a sphere, cylinder and ellipsoid:
  count and touch decided exactly. Against a cone: both nappes; unbounded
  domains through its quadratic, wherever the origin lies. Against a torus:
  within its bounding sphere.
- B-splines, circles and ellipses against all six, conics through exact
  rational quarter-turn arcs, including crossings on the arc cuts (#300).
  A curve lying in a plane is an overlap, clipped to the box.
- Hyperbolas and parabolas against all six (#292), over bounded domains.

Evidence: `tests/intersect/LineSurfaceTests.cpp`, `SplineSurfaceTests.cpp`,
`LineRevolutionTests.cpp`, `ConicSurfaceTests.cpp`, `ConicArcCutTests.cpp`,
`OpenConicSurfaceTests.cpp`, `EllipsePlaneReplayTests.cpp`;
`AdversarialTests.cpp` (the grid of curve kinds and surfaces, every result
validated as geometry or failed closed, under translation, scaling,
reversal and far origins); `IntAna_IntConicQuad` differentials in
`tests/occt/`; `docs/intersection.rst`, *Curves against surfaces*.

**Fails closed by design.**

- `IntersectionFailed`: a cone over an unbounded domain along a generator,
  through the apex or tangent within rounding; a conic along a curved
  surface (a parallel of a sphere, a torus's meridian); a clip within
  rounding of a box edge; a line near tangency to a torus from 2^-40 and at
  an exact touch of the tube's top (`AdversarialTests.cpp`).
- `NotSupported`: a hyperbola or parabola over an unbounded domain.

**Not yet evidenced:** OCCT differentials for conics against the cone,
torus and ellipsoid; a regression corpus; B2.1 timing.

## 4. Curves against B-spline surfaces

**Supported.** Every built-in curve against polynomial and rational
B-spline surfaces, over a requested box. Transverse crossings are isolated
by Krawczyk's operator on C(s) − S(u, v). A crossing on a closed surface's
seam is reported once when the whole direction is requested. A requested
domain ending on an interior knot bounds the search (#318).

Evidence: `tests/intersect/CurveSplineSurfaceTests.cpp`; lines against
B-spline surfaces compared with `GeomAPI_IntCS` (48 lines,
`tests/occt/IntersectionDifferentialTests.cpp`); `docs/intersection.rst`,
*Curves against B-spline surfaces*.

**Fails closed by design** (`IntersectionFailed`):

- tangent contact;
- a curve lying in the surface;
- a crossing too nearly tangential to isolate, or a search past 2^16 pairs;
- a crossing within rounding of a requested domain's end, except on a
  closed seam;
- a requested domain of a single parameter (#318).

**Out of scope or unresolved:** a periodic B-spline surface is not wrapped
beyond its own domain. OCCT differentials beyond lines, and a regression
corpus, do not exist yet.

Note: the manual still says, at the end of *Curves against surfaces*,
"Every other pair reports `NotImplemented`". That is stale since #292 and
the B-spline surface handler: `DispatchTests.cpp` requires every pair to
have a handler.

## 5. Elementary surfaces and the ellipsoid (analytic SSI)

**Supported.** Every pair of plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus and
ellipsoid.

- Plane–plane, plane–cylinder, cylinder–cylinder (skew axes included, with
  certified seam isolation, #322).
- Closed forms for coaxial surfaces of revolution, a sphere touching a
  cylinder, cone or torus off its axis, a plane through a cone's apex, a
  plane containing a torus's axis, a plane against an ellipsoid.
- Every other configuration, the quartics included, through the certified
  parametric–implicit trace.

Evidence: `tests/intersect/ElementaryPairsTests.cpp`,
`ImplicitTraceTests.cpp`, `CylinderCylinderSkewTests.cpp`,
`SkewOrderTests.cpp`, `PlaneEllipsoidTests.cpp`; `corpus/analytic-ssi/`
(1061 cases) and `corpus/curved-ssi/` (622 closed-form cases), every case
in both orders in the weekly Robustness job (`SurfaceCorpusTests.cpp`);
`GeomAPI_IntSS` and `IntAna_QuadQuadGeo` differentials on hand-written
pairs in `tests/occt/`; `docs/intersection.rst`, *Elementary surfaces*,
*Cylinders with skew axes*, *A plane and an ellipsoid*.

**Fails closed by design.**

- `IntersectionFailed`:
  - the trace at an unresolvable box (tangencies of non-coaxial pairs,
    singular points such as the lemniscate's node);
  - a plane through, or within rounding of, an ellipsoid's pole;
  - coaxial cones whose semi-angles differ within 2^-40 in sine;
  - skew-cylinder seam cuts that cannot be separated, or past 2^16
    enclosures (#322).
- `NotImplemented`, each with its message in the code:
  - a coaxial circle that a bounded plane box cuts, which needs a certified
    clip (`ElementaryPairs.cpp`); partial plane–cylinder circles are
    answered (#215);
  - a partial plane–cylinder ellipse in a bounded plane box
    (`PlaneCylinder.cpp`);
  - coincident cones, spheres or tori over part of their charts, or spheres
    whose poles differ (`ElementaryPairs.cpp`);
  - a rotated, bounded plane–plane coincidence (`PlanePlane.cpp`);
  - a pair that may reach infinity along an asymptotic direction (unbounded
    cylinder/cone and cone/cone) without a bounded box on one surface
    (`ImplicitTrace.cpp`);
  - an ellipsoid box outside its base turn (`PlaneEllipsoid.cpp`).

**Listed as remaining in the ledger and not yet declared:** tangent
circles on a sphere whose poles are off the common axis, and closed forms
for coaxial spheroids. Both go to the trace, which fails closed at a
tangency. This draft proposes declaring them fail-closed exclusions, but no
test yet pins their outcome.

**Not yet evidenced:** comparison of the corpus with OCCT (B2.3); OCCT
differentials for the ellipsoid's pairs.

## 6. A B-spline surface against an analytic surface

**Supported.** A B-spline surface, polynomial or rational, against a
plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus or ellipsoid, in either order. The
parametric–implicit trace runs with the B-spline as the carrier, and every
branch is certified.

Evidence: `tests/intersect/SplineAnalyticTests.cpp` (a bilinear B-spline
plane against each analytic surface in closed form, a paraboloid and a
rational quarter cylinder, MR-01 to MR-04); `docs/intersection.rst`, *Two
B-spline surfaces* (last part).

**Fails closed by design** (`IntersectionFailed`): tangencies (some answer
only the contact point); a box the trace cannot resolve; a search past its
budget.

**Not yet evidenced:** a corpus and OCCT differentials for this family.

## 7. Two B-spline surfaces (generic SSI)

**Supported.** Polynomial and positive-weight rational B-spline surface
pairs over finite requested parameter boxes, in either operand order:

- proved separation;
- transverse branches, open and closed, marched and certified within the
  resolution, including far placements (#315) and small circles near a
  rational sphere's pole down to a thousandth of the radius (#317);
- transverse nodes of polynomial and rational surfaces, reported as four
  branches sharing the node.

The minimum reference set is the ledger's *Generic NURBS SSI supported
domain for v0.2*: 58 fixtures from `corpus/nurbs-ssi/`, required to
resolve in both orders.

Evidence: `tests/intersect/GenericSSITests.cpp`, `GenericSSINodeTests.cpp`,
`GenericSSITranslationTests.cpp`, `tests/surface/CertifyTests.cpp`;
`[nurbs-ssi-release]` passed 2339 of 2339 assertions in CI on #317's
branch (run 44886018197). It has not been re-run in CI on main since #318
and #323; #323 reports a local pass on db1706f. `docs/intersection.rst`,
*Two B-spline surfaces*.

**Fails closed by design** (`IntersectionFailed`, naming the patches):

- a tangency along a curve, and an isolated touch;
- any tangency of rational surfaces;
- a branch point not proved a transverse node: a degenerate Hessian
  (three branches through a point), a node on a knot line or a seam
  (`corpus/nurbs-ssi/cylinder-cylinder/node-*`), a node with a gap beyond
  the tolerance;
- an overlap;
- a search past 2^14 pairs;
- a plane's section a ten-thousandth of a rational sphere's radius below
  its pole or nearer, along a knot line, through a corner of four patches,
  or through the pole (`GenericSSITests.cpp`, "a sphere's section that is
  not resolved is refused, never answered in part");
- a requested domain of a single parameter, or a cut that cannot be
  enclosed (#318).

The 32 `may_fail_closed` fixtures of `corpus/nurbs-ssi/` belong to these
classes: specific tangencies, a near-coincident overlap, the smallest loop,
far placements of the saddle node, and the cylinder–cylinder node on a
knot and seam. The weekly exhaustive test holds all 90 fixtures to "no
wrong answer".

**Open in the ledger, proposed here as v0.2 exclusions for the owner to
confirm:**

- tracing tangential branches;
- higher-order singularities;
- nodes on knot lines or seams;
- fitted smooth curves in place of certified polylines;
- parallelism per patch pair (ADR-0007).

**Not yet evidenced:** OCCT differentials for this family; B-spline cases
in the 500-case gate's corpus; triage of `SSI-translated-boundary`, which
#279 repaired but the metamorphic inventory still lists as untriaged.

## 8. Products of intersections (linearisation) and `ClosestApproach`

**Supported** (ADR-0021 §9, stage 4; `docs/sensitivity.rst`):

- residuals, `F_z`, `F_p` and a regularity status at curve–surface points,
  surface–surface branch points (normal-plane tracking constraint) and
  points of two plane curves;
- `IntersectJvp` and `IntersectVjp` for the bindings listed in the
  ledger's *Intersection linearisation* row, including frame rotations;
- `ClosestApproach` for two lines, circles or ellipses over requested
  domains (ADR-0052), its minimum proved global (#312 repaired a
  refinement that stopped one parameter short of its end).

Evidence: `tests/intersect/LinearizationTests.cpp`,
`BranchLinearizationTests.cpp`, `PlanarLinearizationTests.cpp`,
`ClosestApproachTests.cpp`.

**Fails closed by design.**

- Tangencies and overlaps are `Nondifferentiable`; undecided contacts
  `Indeterminate`; grazing crossings `NearEvent`.
- Bindings a curve or surface lacks are refused.
- `ClosestApproach` of B-spline curves is `NotSupported` (ADR-0052
  addendum, decision 5).

**Out of scope (ADR-0052):** derivatives of a meeting of two space curves,
which is `Nondifferentiable` by design.

## 9. The consumer: Booleans on planes and cylinders

v0.2 is the Intersections release. It advertises Booleans only as the
consumer the ledger's *Intersection to Boolean* row tests: solids whose
faces are planes and cylinders, through Union, Common and both Cuts.

Evidence: `tests/boolean/IntegrationTests.cpp` (its `[known]` list
empty), `MeasureTests.cpp` (Steinmetz bicylinder and tricylinder), the
issue #160 drill grids (#319–#321, the large grids in `issue160-large.yml`
only).

**Fails closed by design:** a solid with a sphere or ellipsoid face is
refused (`NotSupported` or `NotImplemented`), and coincident curved faces
are `NotSupported`. Two spheres through every operation return a valid
result or fail closed (#294).

## Out of scope for v0.2

- **Curved Booleans.** Cones, spheres, tori and ellipsoids in Union,
  Common and Cut, and coincident curved faces. The ledger records this as
  the owner's decision of 2026-11-08 ("off the v0.2 critical path"), and
  the v0.2 team brief of 2026-11-22 says "post-v0.2". Two documents on main
  say otherwise: [`curved-booleans.md`](curved-booleans.md) ("curved
  Booleans stay in v0.2 (2026-11-10)", "every case below is required for
  v0.2") and ADR-0048's acceptance line ("the curved Booleans of v0.2 build
  on it"). The owner should confirm which holds, and those documents
  should then be brought in line.
- The OCCT differential of the ellipsoid against a `BRepBuilderAPI_GTransform`
  sphere (ledger, *Native ellipsoid*).
- Certified G01/G02 bounds and the tolerant regression suite (ADR-0050,
  step 0.3-T3).
- General topological event tracking (ADR-0054 is proposed; its stage A is
  not a blanket v0.2 blocker).
- Compilers other than GCC 16 (owner's decision of 2026-11-07). The
  supported platforms are GCC 16 on Linux, macOS and Windows (MSYS2).
