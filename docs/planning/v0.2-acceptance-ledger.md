The line–line slice does not change v0.1 scope or the product version. Its
configuration and domain decisions use exact predicates on the nominal input
doubles. The model resolution limits output rounding only. Numerical inputs
outside the checked expansion domain fail explicitly; automatic rescaling is
not implemented. Overlap endpoints are outward-rounded enclosures.
