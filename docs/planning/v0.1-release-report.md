## Supported domain and its limits

What v0.1 claims, and where each claim stops; the limits are documented beside the operations and carried in the ledger's *Limitations carried into v0.1*.

- **Curves.** Line, Circle, Ellipse, Hyperbola and Parabola in space and in the plane; B-spline and NURBS curves of degree 1 to 25, clamped and unclamped, with the periodic form recognised from the data (`Period` is the domain's width; a curve whose knots lie too far from the origin to resolve its period is refused, audit A2). Trimmed curves over any basis, an arc of a periodic basis across its seam. Evaluation and derivatives of every order; batch evaluation (`EvalAll`) bit-identical to `Eval`; `EvalAllFast`, whose deviation from `Eval` is within a bound that is tested on random curves of every degree, not proved (`docs/nurbs.rst`, *Fast batch evaluation*).
- **Surfaces.** Plane, Cylinder, Cone, Sphere and Torus; B-spline and NURBS surfaces; extrusions and revolutions of any profile; trimmed surfaces. B-spline surfaces have no periodic form: a surface is evaluated inside its domain only.
- **Projection.** Global, with certified exclusions; B-spline surface projection is not certified inside its final sub-patches (`docs/nurbs.rst`, *Surface projection*).
- **Fitting.** Interpolation, least squares and approximation to a budget, open and periodic, with or without end derivatives; the deviation is measured at the data points only (`docs/fitting.rst`).
- **Properties.** Frenet frame, curvature and torsion; arc length with an error estimate that is reported, not a certified bound; the parameter at a length (`docs/properties.rst`).
- **Persistence.** Format version 3 of `.stn`, versions 1 and 2 still read; the geometry subset with its numerical policy, not shapes (`docs/data_exchange.rst`).
- **Numerical policy.** Purpose-specific tolerances (ADR-0028); every residual, convergence flag and error estimate is returned, not assumed.
- **Performance claims.** The B1.1–B1.6 targets are met on the reference CPU, an AMD EPYC 7763 (*Benchmark gates*). On other hosted runners some are not: on Intel Xeon, `EvalAllFast` reaches about 1.8× OCCT's grid evaluator against its ≥ 2× target and degree elevation about 0.8× against parity; on an AMD EPYC 9V74 (the run of the candidate), `EvalAllFast` on the rational cubic reaches 1.994× and principal curvatures 0.899, each just under its threshold. The results are correct on every CPU; only these speed claims are scoped.

## Structured failures

Every operation returns `Result<T>`; a failure is a `KernelError` carrying one of the codes of `src/core/Error.cppm`: `InvalidInput`, `InvalidResult`, `ToleranceExceeded`, `IntersectionFailed`, `CertificationFailed`, `DegenerateResult`, `NotSupported`, `NotImplemented`, `ParseError`, `Unresolved`. Data from outside the kernel passes the checked factories (ADR-0006); the kernel's own rearrangements publish through the same checks and return `NotSupported` where no valid result exists (audit A3–A5). Contract violations are a separate class, enforced in the debug build and observed over the whole suite nightly (ADR-0016).

## Benchmark gates

From the run of record, [41424965507](https://github.com/onurtuncer/Stereon/actions/runs/41424965507) on `91dff8e` (2026-11-02, AMD EPYC 7763), B1.1–B1.6 (`docs/benchmarks.md`), decided by `tools/check_release_benchmarks.py` on full-precision medians: verdict **PASS**. Ratio = OCCT / Stereon, within 10 % of 1 is parity.

- **Met:** single-point curve and surface evaluation (1.03 to 3.5), `EvalAllFast` against `GeomGridEval_BSplineCurve` 2.27 and 2.32 (≥ 2× target), grid evaluation 3.3 and 3.4, curve and surface projection 3.6 and 1.25, interpolation 1.76, Bezier decomposition 1.44, degree elevation 1.28, 100 knot insertions 2.3, arc length 0.98 (parity), Frenet frame 1.44, parameter at a length 1.54, principal curvatures and directions 0.95 (within the parity band since #171; 0.90 on 30 October).
- **Met:** B1.3's convergence on the declared corpus `b13-v1`, measured in the run: 20 000 of 20 000 cases converged, no incorrect success, one-sided 95 % lower bound 0.99985. The incorrect success the corpus found on 2026-11-01 (surface case 9441, a point on a knot line 1.37e-3 from the query while one 1.32e-4 away lay inside the neighbouring patch) was repaired by #180.
- **Report only:** the bit-identical `EvalAll`, 0.42 and 0.50; approximation to a budget, 24, not like for like (OCCT's `GeomAPI_PointsToBSpline` also optimises parameters and continuity).
- **A limitation of v0.1, on Intel runners:** the workflow does not choose the hosted runner's CPU, and on both Intel runs of the same code two gates fail by a similar margin: `EvalAllFast` 1.79 and 1.77 and degree elevation 0.78 on [41434476419](https://github.com/onurtuncer/Stereon/actions/runs/41434476419) (`ab41d1a`, Xeon 8573C), 1.86 and 1.74 and 0.79 on [41418855001](https://github.com/onurtuncer/Stereon/actions/runs/41418855001) (`2219942`, Xeon 6975P-C). The owner decided on 2026-11-02 to keep the AMD run as the run of record and to release v0.1 with these claims scoped to the AMD EPYC 7763: on Intel they are not met, and the cause is to be profiled after the release (*Supported domain and its limits*). The candidate's own run, on an AMD EPYC 9V74, misses two rows by less than 0.5 %: `EvalAllFast` on the rational cubic at 1.994 and principal curvatures at 0.899 (*Qualification*).
- **Phase 0 rows in the same run (not v0.1 gates):** B0.2's direct calls lead OCCT's `EvalD0`; `VisitBuiltin` is within 1.10× of the same loop on a local copy for every curve (1.00, 1.00, 1.00), its ratio to the direct call reported (1.23× for the cubic B-spline, an end-to-end cost not yet attributed); the virtual fallback, restated by the owner on 2026-11-02 as at most 5 ns per call over the same local copy, adds 0.00, 0.22 and 0.91 ns, its excess over the direct call reported (6.16 ns for the cubic B-spline, the same unattributed gap), which closes the Phase 0 exit-gate item; it also passes on the Intel run (0.15, 0.27, 0.38 ns). ## Migration from 0.0.x

0.1.0 is the first tagged milestone. The 0.0.x versions were explicitly experimental (roadmap, *Versioning and release gates*, 2), so there is no tagged API to migrate from, and no compatibility promise is made across 0.1.0 either: before 1.0 an incompatible change takes a minor version, with notes like these.

- **Native files.** 0.1.0 writes format version 3 of `.stn` and reads versions 1, 2 and 3 (`tests/io/data/geometry-v1.stn` to `geometry-v3.stn` are kept). Files of every 0.0.x release that wrote geometry read unchanged. The format version is independent of the product version (roadmap, 5).
- **Source.** Code written against 0.0.6 builds against 0.1.0 unchanged: 0.1.0 is 0.0.6 with the release records completed, the benchmark release gate restated (ADR-0003, #183) and the API reference rewritten (#183).
- **The version.** `LibraryVersion` reads 0.1.0; a program that checked for 0.0.x has to accept it.
