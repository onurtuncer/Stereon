# Curve–surface corpus

Curves crossing surfaces at a known point, generated by `tools/generate_curve_surface_corpus.py`. Lint checks with `--check` that the files match the generator.

Each family pairs a curve kind (line, circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola, a cubic B-spline `bspline` and a rational quadratic B-spline `nurbs`) with a surface kind (plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, ellipsoid, a bicubic B-spline patch `bspline` and a rational biquadratic patch `nurbs`). On the surface, a point P is taken at chart parameters away from seams and poles, with its unit normal n, both from the surface's defining formula evaluated by the generator, not by the kernel. The curve is moved rigidly so that its point at a stated parameter is P and its tangent there makes a stated angle with the tangent plane.

| Case name | Angle to the tangent plane |
| --- | --- |
| `normal` | 90 degrees |
| `oblique` | 20 degrees |
| `grazing` | 1 degree |

Inputs are rounded to 1e-12, so a crossing moves by about that over the sine of its angle (6e-11 at 1 degree).

The claim of a case is local. The result has a decided crossing within 1e-9 (per unit of scale) of `expected.point`, at the curve parameter `expected.parameter`. The pair may also meet elsewhere. Every point the result reports must lie on the curve, in the requested domain, and on the surface at its reported parameters.

A case with `expected.may_fail_closed` may instead fail with `IntersectionFailed`; its `expected.known_limit` says why. When the corpus was added (main at 898ed69) 15 cases were so marked, all at 1 degree: every curve kind against both B-spline surfaces, where the search spends its subdivision budget, and a line against the cone.

`tests/intersect/CurveSurfaceCorpusTests.cpp` runs every case.
