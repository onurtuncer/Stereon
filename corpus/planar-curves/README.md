# Planar curve corpus

Pairs of plane curves, generated by `tools/generate_planar_corpus.py`, for the v0.2 ledger's *2D curve–curve* row. Lint checks with `--check` that the files match the generator (`tools: planar corpus is current` in CTest).

Each family is an unordered pair of the kinds `line` (`Line2d`), `circle`, `ellipse`, `hyperbola`, `parabola`, `spline` (a polynomial `BSplineCurve2d`) and `rational` (a rational one): 28 families. Each configuration is placed three ways, `origin`, `far` (translated by (393216, -262144)) and `rotated` (the similarity [[3, -4], [4, 3]], a rotation by atan(4/3) and a scale of 5, then translated by (12, -7)), at two scales, `x1` and `x100`.

## How the answer is known

Every curve but a line is symmetric about its local x axis, with a vertex on it, and satisfies Y = q(x) or x = p(Y) with Y = y² and q, p of degree at most two: circles, ellipses, hyperbolas and parabolas; the quadratic spline x = a t², y = w t (knots inserted at a quarter and three quarters); the quartic spline x = a t² + b t⁴, y = w t over t in [-3, 3], whose poles are then integers; and rational quadratic arcs with integer or dyadic weights that are exactly a circle (weights 5, 3, 5, since the half angle has cosine 3/5), an ellipse (that circle stretched) or a hyperbola (weights 1, 5/4, 1). Two such curves meet where a quadratic in x or in Y vanishes, and a line where a quadratic in its parameter does. The generator solves these in Q(√D) with the input doubles' exact values, so a discriminant or the sign of Y at a root is decided exactly. A root with Y > 0 gives two mirror points of its multiplicity m, one with Y = 0 a point on the axis of multiplicity 2m. Odd multiplicity is a crossing, even a tangency. Restrictions to a branch, an arc's end or a knot are float comparisons that the generator asserts are at least 2⁻¹² of the local size away from deciding. Nothing comes from the kernel.

Inputs are exact doubles at every placement, with one exception. The frame (3/5, 4/5) of an ellipse, a hyperbola or a parabola rounds at `rotated`, so the regimes whose answer a rounding could change (a tangency, an osculation or a coincidence with such a conic) are generated at `origin` and `far` only. A circle keeps the identity frame everywhere, because its point set does not depend on the frame.

## Regimes

| Case name | Configuration | `expected` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `crossing-*` | B's vertex moved 5/4 into A, or a slanted line through A | the crossings |
| `tangent-*` | the vertices meet, the curves on either side (a line: the vertex's tangent) | one tangency of order 2 |
| `tangent-internal-*` | the vertices meet, both on one side, curvatures differ | one tangency of order 2 |
| `osculating-*` | the vertices meet, one side, equal curvature: contact of order 4 | one tangency of order 4 |
| `apart-*` | the `tangent` pair 3·2⁻⁴⁰ apart, relative to the case's magnitude | no point: a proved separation |
| `near-*` | the same distance the other way | two crossings, about 10⁻⁶ of the size apart |
| others (`overlapping-*`, `along*`, `identical-arcs`, `half-turn-arcs`, `reversed-arcs`, `round-ellipse`, `refined-copy`) | the same curve, or arcs of it under the requested domains | the overlapping stretch's ends |

The 3·2⁻⁴⁰ is applied as a shift of B along the local x axis of 3·2⁻⁴⁰ times the power of two above the case's coordinates (`inputs.magnitude`), divided by the scale, so that it stays representable at `far`.

## Format

`inputs.curves` holds two curves named `A` and `B`, each with `type` and the data of its factory: `line` (`origin`, `direction`), `circle` (`center`, `radius`, `x_axis`, `y_axis`), `ellipse` and `hyperbola` (`center`, `major`, `minor`, `x_axis`, `y_axis`), `parabola` (`vertex`, `focal`, `x_axis`, `y_axis`), `bspline` (`degree`, `knots`, `poles`, optional `weights`), and an optional `domain` [lo, hi] for the requested parameter domain; without it the curve's own domain is requested. `inputs.magnitude` is the power of two above every placed coordinate.

`expected.points` lists every point with its `position`, `contact` (`crossing` or `tangential`), multiplicity `order` (1, 2 or 4) and `within`, how far the kernel's point may be from it: a few hundred roundings of the magnitude over the sine of the crossing angle for a crossing, 2⁸ √(u F) for order 2 and 2⁴ (u F³)^¼ for order 4, with u the rounding of the magnitude and F the feature size. Points closer than four times their reach are refused by the generator. `expected.overlaps` lists each overlap's `from` and `to` ends and its `within`. `expected.decided` is true for the pair kinds whose every decision the ledger calls exact (a line against a line or a conic, two circles); their points must come `Decided`. `expected.may_fail_closed` names the ledger's documented limitation when the kernel may refuse the case with `IntersectionFailed` or `NotImplemented` instead of answering. It never permits a wrong answer.

`tests/intersect/PlanarCorpusTests.cpp` runs one placement of each configuration (the rotated one at scale 1, or the far one where no rotated one exists) in every pull request, and every case in both orders in the weekly Robustness job (`[exhaustive]`).
